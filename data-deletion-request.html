Data Deletion Request

Essential on the Go is committed to protecting your privacy and providing you with control over your personal data. This page explains how you can request the deletion of your account and associated data.

⚠️ Important Notice

Before requesting data deletion, please understand that this action is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to all your data, including:

  • Booking history and trip records
  • Chat messages and communications
  • Profile information and preferences
  • Community membership and notification settings

How to Request Data Deletion

Step-by-Step Process:

  1. Open the Essential on the Go app on your device
  2. Log in to your account using your email and password
  3. Access the menu by tapping the menu icon (☰) in the top-right corner
  4. Select "Request Data Deletion" from the menu options
  5. Review the information about what data will be deleted
  6. Confirm your request by following the on-screen instructions

📧 Alternative Contact Method

What Data We Collect and Delete

Data Type What We Collect Deletion Status Retention Period
Account Information Name, email, phone number, user type (client/business/driver) ✅ Deleted Immediate
Profile Data Profile photos, business information, address details ✅ Deleted Immediate
Booking & Trip Data Booking history, trip details, pickup/delivery locations ✅ Deleted Immediate
Communication Data Chat messages, notifications, support communications ✅ Deleted Immediate
Location Data GPS coordinates, location history, route information ✅ Deleted Immediate
Device Information Device ID, app version, operating system ✅ Deleted Immediate
Advertising Data Advertising identifier, ad interactions and consent choices, collected by Google AdMob Not stored by us — managed by Google Per Google's policies; reset or withdraw consent in Privacy options in the App
Usage Analytics App usage patterns, feature interactions ✅ Deleted Immediate

We do not collect or store payment card data. The App is free to use; if you bought the optional ad-free subscription, the payment itself is processed and held by Google Play. The fields above are removed from our active systems once your request is processed, which we complete within 30 days and usually much sooner.

Data We Retain (Legal Requirements)

The following data may be retained for legal and regulatory purposes:

  • App Store Purchase Records: If you bought the optional ad-free subscription, the purchase record is held by Google Play under Google's own policies and is not part of your account data with us — we only store your subscription status, which is deleted with the account
  • Legal Compliance: Data related to ongoing legal proceedings or investigations
  • Security Logs: Access logs and security-related data for up to 90 days
  • Backup Data: Encrypted backup data may be retained for up to 30 days

What Happens After Deletion

Immediate Effects:

  • Your account will be permanently deactivated
  • You will lose access to all app features
  • All personal data will be removed from our active systems
  • You will no longer receive notifications or communications

Processing Time:

  • Account Deactivation: Immediate
  • Data Deletion: Within 30 days
  • Backup Cleanup: Within 60 days
  • Confirmation Email: Sent within 7 days

Recreating Your Account

If you change your mind after deletion, you can create a new account using a different email address. However, please note that:

  • You cannot recover your previous data
  • You will need to provide all information again
  • Your previous booking history and preferences will not be restored
  • You may need to re-verify your identity
