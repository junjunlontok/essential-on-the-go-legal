Privacy Policy for Essential on the Go

Effective Date: August 13, 2025
Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Essential on the Go ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). The App connects clients with local businesses for various services including laundry, transportation, and other essential services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using our App, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, password
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, address, user preferences
  • Business Information (for Business Users): Business name, description, contact details, business hours, services offered, business location
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through our in-app chat system, customer support communications

2.2 Location Information

  • Precise Location: GPS coordinates when you use location-based features (with your permission)
  • Approximate Location: General area information for service matching
  • Address Information: Pickup and delivery addresses for bookings

2.3 Usage and Technical Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • App Usage: Features used, time spent in app, interaction patterns
  • Technical Data: IP address, app version, crash reports, performance data
  • Firebase Analytics: App usage statistics and performance metrics (anonymized)
  • Push Notification Data: A device messaging token, so notifications you have enabled can be delivered

2.4 Optional Ad-Free Subscription

The App is free and there is no paid plan for access. The only thing we sell is an optional subscription that removes advertising for the account that buys it. If you purchase it, Google Play Billing processes the payment and we receive only your subscription status (active, cancelled or expired) and a purchase token — never your card number or billing address. We do not process payments between clients and businesses, and we do not store payment card details.

2.5 Advertising Information

  • Ad Interaction Data: Ads viewed, clicked, or interacted with
  • Advertising ID: The device advertising identifier, used by Google AdMob to personalise ads where you have consented
  • Consent Choices: The decision you make in the Google-certified consent form, so the App knows whether it may request ads and personalise them
  • Ad Performance: Aggregated effectiveness metrics for ad campaigns

Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data are collected by Google, not stored by us. If you decline, or have not yet consented, only non-personalised ads are requested.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Core App Functionality

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Connect clients with nearby businesses
  • Facilitate booking and service requests
  • Enable in-app messaging between users
  • Manage the optional ad-free subscription status reported by Google Play Billing
  • Provide customer support

3.2 Service Improvement

  • Analyze app usage to improve features and performance
  • Conduct research and analytics to enhance user experience
  • Develop new features and services
  • Troubleshoot technical issues

3.3 Communication

  • Send service-related notifications
  • Provide updates about the optional ad-free subscription
  • Respond to customer support inquiries
  • Send promotional communications (with consent)

3.4 Advertising

  • Display the advertisements that keep the App free for everyone
  • Request personalised ads only where you have consented, and non-personalised ads otherwise
  • Measure ad effectiveness and performance
  • Stop showing ads for an account that bought the optional ad-free subscription, or while a rewarded ad-free pass is active

3.5 Legal and Safety

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect against fraud and abuse
  • Ensure user safety and security

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Service Providers

We may share information with third-party service providers:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, file storage, analytics, crash reporting, remote config and push messaging
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform: Advertising and ad consent management
  • Google Play Billing: Payment processing for the optional ad-free subscription
  • Google Maps, Places and Location services: Address search, maps and distance calculation for bookings
  • Facebook Login (Meta): Optional sign-in with a Facebook account
  • Cloud hosting providers: Data storage and app infrastructure

4.2 Business Transfers

In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred to the new entity.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights and safety.

4.4 Consent-Based Sharing

We may share information with your explicit consent for specific purposes.

4.5 What We Don't Share

  • We do not sell personal information to third parties
  • We do not share information for commercial purposes without consent
  • Transaction details between clients and businesses remain private (we only facilitate connections)

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication systems
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and monitoring
  • Firebase security rules and protocols

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information, except where retention is required by law. You can start a deletion request from the App menu under Request Data Deletion, or by following the steps on our Data Deletion Request page; verified requests are completed within 30 days. Purchase records for the optional ad-free subscription are held by Google Play and are not part of your account data.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Account Management

  • Access and update your profile information
  • Change privacy settings
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

7.2 Location Services

  • Enable or disable location sharing
  • Control location precision settings

7.3 Communications

  • Opt out of promotional communications
  • Manage notification preferences

7.4 Advertising

  • Change or withdraw your ad consent at any time under Privacy options in the App's account screen
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ad Settings
  • Buy the optional ad-free subscription, or watch a rewarded ad to pause ads for 30 minutes — neither is ever required to use a feature

7.5 Data Portability

Request a copy of your personal data in a portable format.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete such information.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

10. Third-Party Services

Our App integrates with third-party services:

  • Google services: Firebase, AdMob with the User Messaging Platform, Play Billing, Maps, Places and Play services
  • Payment processor: Google Play, for the optional ad-free subscription only
  • Meta: Facebook Login, when you choose to sign in with a Facebook account

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. Google's is at policies.google.com/privacy, and its advertising controls are at myadcenter.google.com.
